A Field Guide to Tides

The tide is the slow breathing of the sea. Twice a day the water climbs the beach and twice a day it draws back, following a rhythm set far away by the moon.

Our spring tide charts show the highest water of the month, while the neap tide notes explain the quiet in between. The old interactive tide wheel no longer works, but the printed tables do.

Watch the line of seaweed on the sand. It marks where the water turned, and it is the only chart you need on a clear day.
