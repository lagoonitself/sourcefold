# Turbocharger

> Saved from [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbocharger) on 2026-10-15. Wikipedia articles change; check the live page for edits.

In an [internal combustion engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_combustion_engine), a **turbocharger** is a [forced induction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forced_induction) device that compresses the intake air.[^1][^2]

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/42/Turbocharger_Animation_by_Tyroola.gif)

## History

The 1905 patent by Alfred Büchi is often considered the birth of the turbocharger.[^2] Boost pressure follows $p_{2}/p_{1}$, or in full:

$$
\eta = \frac {T_{1}}{T_{2}}
$$

### Early use

-   Aircraft engines
-   Trucks
