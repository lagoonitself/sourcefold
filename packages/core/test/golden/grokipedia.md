# Artificial General Intelligence

> Saved from [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Artificial_General_Intelligence) on 2026-10-15. Grokipedia articles change; check the live page for edits.

## Overview

Artificial general intelligence (AGI) is the hypothetical ability of an intelligent agent to understand or learn any intellectual task that a human being can.
