Calm stays the default

Calm is today's app, unchanged. Lively is a switch in Settings, Appearance, Motion. Reduced-motion settings on your computer always win.

Same look, app and extension

The extension moves to the app's ink-and-highlighter colours and fonts in both modes, and gets the same springs, ripples and pet in Lively.

What Lively adds

Springy dialogs and tabs, the highlighter drawing under hovered titles, a soft press ripple, rows that bounce in, paper scraps on save, rolling counts, and a pet that blinks, follows your pointer, dozes and cheers.
